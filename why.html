Why is this important?

This isn't exactly an uncommon critique. AI was created to help medical professionals diagnose serious medical conditions with far improved accuracy than humans could, and the results for that show.

Unfortunately, AI tech conglomerates have taken to using as many environmental resources as possible to power solutions that could just as easily be powered by the user, which we will show is possible to leverage in these tutorials.

In addition, many of the ideas that become usable end up producing ethically and morally questionable output, such as replacing artists in favor of less-than-ideal generic copycats, and otherwise illegal imagery. This site, as a side goal, will aim to give you better principles of AI data collection and AI idea design to prevent these issues.

The tools we will be using specifically for this purpose include but are not limited to:

ONNX.js
Vanilla HTML, CSS, and Javascript in the browser
Python (strictly for training, but I recommend at least one version below the current numbered version i.e. 3.13 if it's 3.14, possibly two versions down)
Required Python Libraries: Ultralytics, ONNX
A High-end (yet still consumer grade) Consumer Desktop PC. Think like a GPU with 16GB VRAM and 128 GB DDR4 or DDR5 RAM (with current CPU cache there's not noticable enough difference between the two at the moment), and at least a good quality 16 core processor.
(optional but recommended) CUDA or AMD/Intel/Apple Equivalent and Pytorch to speed up Training
